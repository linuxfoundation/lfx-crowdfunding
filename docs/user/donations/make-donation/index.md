> **Sign in required** — You must be signed in with your [LF ID](https://openprofile.dev/) to make a donation.

## Starting a donation

Open any published initiative page and click the **Donate** button. A drawer will open with the donation form.

## Step 1 — Choose an amount and frequency

![Donation amount selection](/images/docs/donation-step-amount.png)

Select a preset amount or enter a custom amount in the input field.

Then choose your donation frequency:

- **One-time** — a single charge processed immediately
- **Monthly** — a recurring charge on the same date each month

Optionally, you can allocate your donation to a specific funding goal (e.g. Development, Marketing, Travel) if the initiative has defined funding categories. By default your donation goes toward all project needs.

If the initiative offers [sponsorship tiers](../../initiatives/sponsorship-tiers/), you'll see a set of tier cards (e.g. Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze) instead — select one to set your amount to that tier's goal, or choose a custom amount below the tiers.

## Step 2 — Choose who you're donating as

![Choosing who you're donating as](/images/docs/screenshots/donations/make-donation-donating-as.png)

Under **I'm donating as**, pick one:

- **Individual** — the donation is credited to you, with your name and avatar.
- **An organization** — the donation is credited to a company or organization.

To donate on behalf of an organization, select **An organization** and search for it by name. Results show each organization's name and website. Pick the right one; its name and website then appear in the organization row.

If your organization isn't in the results, click **Can't find it? Enter it manually** and type:

- **Organization name**, e.g. Acme Inc.
- **Website**, e.g. acme.com

Click **Search instead** to go back to the search.

A few things to know:

- The organization is shown publicly as you entered or selected it. There is no check that you work there.
- You can't change the organization after the donation is made, and confirmation emails don't mention it.
- For a monthly donation, every charge is credited to the organization you chose when you subscribed.

## Step 3 — Payment

![Donation payment step](/images/docs/donation-step-payment.png)

Credit card is the only accepted payment method. Paying by invoice is not supported.

Enter your card details:

- Card number
- Expiry date
- CVC

If you have previously saved a card, it will be shown here. You can use it directly or click **Use a different card** to enter new details.

**Order summary** — The drawer shows a breakdown of your donation amount and total. The Linux Foundation underwrites all transaction fees, so 100% of your donation goes to the initiative.

Click **Donate** to process your payment. For cards that require additional verification (3D Secure — an extra authentication step your bank may require), you may be prompted to authenticate before the payment completes.

## Anonymous donations

LFX Crowdfunding does not currently offer a donor-selectable anonymous option. Your name and avatar, or the organization's name if you donated on behalf of one, will be displayed publicly on the initiative's backer list. If your donor information is unavailable at the time of processing, your name will appear as **Anonymous** on the initiative page.

## After donating

![Donation confirmation screen](/images/docs/screenshots/donations/make-donation-confirmation.png)

Once your payment is processed, you will see a confirmation screen with the initiative name and amount. From there you can share your donation on X or LinkedIn, or go to [My Donations on LFX](https://app.lfx.dev/crowdfunding/donations) to view your giving history.

An email acknowledgement is sent automatically after each successful payment.
