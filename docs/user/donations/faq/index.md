Answers to the most common questions about donating on LFX Crowdfunding.

## Is there a minimum donation amount?

There is no enforced minimum for one-time donations. Monthly recurring donations must be at least $1 per month.

## What payment methods are accepted?

Donations can only be paid by credit card, processed securely through Stripe. Paying by invoice is not supported, and neither are bank transfers or other payment methods. See [Payment Account](../../payment-account/) to manage your saved card.

## Do I need an account to donate?

Yes. You must be signed in with an [LF ID](https://openprofile.dev/) account to complete a donation.

## Can I donate on behalf of my company or organization?

Yes. In the donate drawer, under **I'm donating as**, select **An organization** and search for it by name. If it isn't in the results, click **Can't find it? Enter it manually** and enter its name and website. The organization's name then appears on the initiative's backer list instead of yours. See **Step 2 — Choose who you're donating as** in [Making a Donation](../make-donation/) for full steps.

## Who can see that I donated on behalf of an organization?

The initiative's backer list shows the organization's name publicly, with donor type **Company**. Your own name isn't shown there. People who can view the organization in LFX see your name and email next to the donation on its LFX organization page, unless you check **Donate anonymously**; then they see **Anonymous**.

## Do I need to work at the organization to donate on its behalf?

There is no check that you work at the organization. The organization is shown as you selected or entered it, so make sure you have permission to donate in its name.

## Can I change the organization after donating?

No. The organization can't be changed after the donation is made. Your confirmation email shows which organization the donation was credited to. For a monthly donation, every charge is credited to the organization you chose when you subscribed; to credit a different organization, cancel the subscription and start a new donation.

## Can my organization pay by invoice or bank transfer?

No. Organization donations are paid by credit card, the same as individual donations. Paying by invoice and bank transfer are not supported.

## Are transaction fees deducted from my donation?

No. The Linux Foundation underwrites all transaction fees, so 100% of your donation goes to the initiative.

## Can I donate anonymously?

Yes. You still sign in, but in the donate drawer you can check **Donate anonymously**. Your donation then appears with its amount but your name shows as **Anonymous** to everyone else, including the initiative's owner. If you donate on behalf of an organization, the organization's name still shows. You can change this later from [My Donations on LFX](https://app.lfx.dev/crowdfunding/donations). See **Anonymous donations** in [Making a Donation](../make-donation/) for details.

## How do I cancel a monthly donation?

Go to [My Donations on LFX](https://app.lfx.dev/crowdfunding/donations), find the active subscription, and select **Cancel**. Cancellation takes effect immediately and you will not be charged again. See [Managing Your Donations](../manage-donations/) for full steps.

## Can I get a refund?

Donations are processed immediately and are generally non-refundable. If you believe a charge was made in error, contact the Linux Foundation support team.

## Is my donation tax-deductible?

All donations are made to the Linux Foundation, a 501(c)(6) non-profit organisation (a US non-profit category). Tax deductibility depends on your jurisdiction — consult your tax adviser.

## Why was my card declined?

Common reasons include incorrect card details, insufficient funds, or a 3D Secure (an extra authentication step your bank may require) challenge that was not completed. Try re-entering your card details or contact your bank.
