Donating to an initiative supports open source projects directly. All payments are processed securely through Stripe.

> **Sign in required** — Making a donation requires an [LF ID](https://openprofile.dev/) account. You will be prompted to log in before your payment is processed.

## Making a donation

1. Open any published [initiative](../initiatives/) page
2. Click the **Donate** button
3. Choose a donation amount or enter a custom amount
4. Select **One-time** or **Monthly** recurring
5. Under **I'm donating as**, choose **Individual** or **An organization**
6. Enter your payment details and confirm

For a detailed walkthrough, see [Making a Donation](./make-donation/).

## Donating as an organization

You can donate on behalf of a company or organization instead of as an individual. In the donate drawer, under **I'm donating as**, select **An organization**, then search for the organization by name and pick it from the results. If it isn't listed, click **Can't find it? Enter it manually** and type the organization's name and website.

The donation is then credited to the organization: its name appears on the initiative's backer list with donor type **Company**. A few things to know:

- There is no check that you work at the organization you pick.
- People who can view the organization in LFX see your name and email next to the donation on its LFX organization page.
- You can't change the organization after the donation is made.
- For a monthly donation, every charge is credited to the organization you chose when you subscribed.

Payment is still by credit card. For full details, see **Step 2 — Choose who you're donating as** in [Making a Donation](./make-donation/).

## One-time vs. monthly donations

**One-time** donations are a single charge processed immediately.

**Monthly** donations are charged on the same date each month. You can cancel at any time — see [Managing Your Donations](./manage-donations/).

## Donation history and managing subscriptions

Your past donations and active monthly subscriptions are managed through the LFX platform. See [Managing Your Donations](./manage-donations/) for details.

## Email acknowledgement and tax information

All donations are made to the Linux Foundation, a 501(c)(6) non-profit organisation (a category of non-profit under US tax law). An email acknowledgement is sent automatically after each payment. For tax purposes, please consult your tax adviser regarding the deductibility of your donations.

## Payment security

Payments are processed by [Stripe](https://stripe.com). LFX Crowdfunding never stores your full card details.

Donations can only be paid by credit card. Paying by invoice is not supported.

## After completing

Once your donation is processed you will receive an email acknowledgement. You can view your giving history and manage subscriptions at [My Donations on LFX](https://app.lfx.dev/crowdfunding/donations).
