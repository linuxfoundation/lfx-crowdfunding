Managing your donations lets you view your giving history, choose whether your name is shown with each donation, and cancel any active monthly subscriptions on LFX Crowdfunding.

## Before you begin

You must have completed at least one donation. Sign in with your [LF ID](https://openprofile.dev/) before proceeding.

## Steps

1. Go to [My Donations on LFX](https://app.lfx.dev/crowdfunding/donations).

### View your donation history

![Donation history list](/images/docs/screenshots/donations/manage-donations-list.png)

2. Review your past donations. Each entry shows the initiative name, amount, date, and payment status. Donations you made anonymously are marked **Anonymous**.

### Hide or show your name

![Hide or show your name on a donation](/images/docs/screenshots/donations/manage-donations-anonymous.png)

You can change whether a donation is anonymous at any time:

3. Find the donation in your list.
4. Choose **Hide my name** to make it anonymous, or **Show my name** to show your name again.

For a monthly donation, you can also open it and use **Hide my name** or **Show my name** under **Your Name**. The change applies to every charge of that subscription, past and future.

The initiative page and the organization's LFX organization page reflect the change right away. Older donations, and donations recorded outside card payments, can't be changed here. See **Anonymous donations** in [Making a Donation](../make-donation/) for what anonymity hides.

### Cancel a recurring donation

![Cancel subscription](/images/docs/screenshots/donations/manage-donations-cancel.png)

5. Find the active monthly subscription you want to cancel.
6. Select **Cancel** next to the subscription.
7. Confirm the cancellation when prompted.

## After completing

Cancellation takes effect immediately — you will not be charged again. The subscription will no longer appear in your active subscriptions list.
